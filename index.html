Mediterranean Quinoa Bowl

A nutritious bowl packed with protein and fresh vegetables

25 min 2 servings Easy
420 calories

Ingredients

  • 1 cup quinoa
  • 2 cups vegetable broth
  • 1 can chickpeas, drained
  • 2 cups cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 1 cucumber, diced
  • 1/4 red onion, sliced
  • 1/4 cup feta cheese
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • Lemon juice and herbs

Instructions

  1. Cook quinoa in vegetable broth according to package directions
  2. Roast chickpeas with olive oil at 400°F for 20 minutes
  3. Chop all vegetables and prepare toppings
  4. Assemble bowls with quinoa as base
  5. Top with roasted chickpeas, vegetables, and feta
  6. Drizzle with olive oil and lemon juice
